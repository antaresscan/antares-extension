HOW TO PICK FROM HERE

  1. Smallest possible signal? Pick A — Logo only. No border, no label. Just the colour shift carries everything.
  2. Most explicit? Pick B — Logo + label. Logo + “LIFE”/“FREE”. No ambiguity.
  3. Coin / token feel? Pick C — Pill border or I — Concentric ring. Logo framed.
  4. Premium feel without animation? Pick D — Halo or J — Engraved plate. Static but visually rich.
  5. Subtle motion? Pick E — Pulse. 2.4s breathing animation, not distracting.
  6. Bold / brand-forward? Pick F — Logo replaces wordmark. Big logo, no “ANTARES” text.
  7. Magazine / luxury? Pick K — Logo + italic serif. Playfair Display italic looks editorial / wedding-invitation.
  8. Personal / status? Pick H — Member ID. Adds a number, feels like a wristband.
My pick : D — Halo as the cleanest visual statement (logo + soft gold glow, nothing else needed) ; or I — Concentric ring if you want the “sealed coin” / signet feel ; or J — Engraved plate for the most physically luxurious feel.
