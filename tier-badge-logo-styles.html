HOW TO PICK A WATERMARK

Topbar badge is locked in: A — bare gold logo. Now the question is what (if anything) sits behind the overlay content.

  1. Want nothing extra? Pick M0 — none. Topbar logo is the only chrome. Cleanest possible.
  2. Smallest possible motion? Pick M1 — small rotating. 60px eye, 30s rotation. The smallest sign of life.
  3. Calm, premium centerpiece? Pick M3 — big gold rotating. 220px gold eye, 70s rotation. Imposing but smooth.
  4. Lots of motion, multiple logos? Pick M9 — constellation or M14 — galaxy. 5 little logos drifting in their own rhythm.
  5. Most cinematic? Pick M12 — gold rotation + shimmer. Big gold eye + champagne sweep across.
  6. Mechanical / heart-beat? Pick M13 — counter-pulse. Two stacked logos pulsing in alternation.
  7. Atomic / orbital? Pick M8 — orbital atom. Static gold centre + 2 satellite eyes orbiting.
  8. Vibe of falling stars? Pick M10 — cascade. Eyes endlessly raining top to bottom.
  9. Logo dans le coin haut-droit ? M15 petit static, M16 or qui tourne 25s, M17 oversize bleed, M18 or pulse, M19 or qui tourne 40s.
My picks (top 3):
M1 — small rotating for the most subtle “something’s alive” signal that never imposes.
M9 — constellation for variety + multiple logos drifting at different speeds (your “multi-logo” brief).
M14 — galaxy for a premium centerpiece (gold star) surrounded by 4 dust-like satellites.
