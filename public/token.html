SAFE GATE

Antares applies a SAFE gate: even if a token scores ≥ 800/1000, it cannot display SAFE if any of the following are true:

  • ■ Chart pattern risk detected (L7)
  • ■ Identity / copycat risk detected (L6)
  • ■ LP not burned or locked (L2)

In this case the verdict is capped at CAUTION minimum.
