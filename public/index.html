The Safe Gate

Why SAFE is hard to get

Most scanners only check contract flags. Antares adds a SAFE gate: even scoring ≥ 800/1000, a token cannot display SAFE if any of these are detected:

Chart pattern risk (L6) — Crashcoin, parabolic launch, blow-off top, wash trading…
Identity / copycat risk (L5) — Brand imitation, suspicious naming (TRUMP2, PEPE2, V2…)
LP not secured (L2) — Liquidity neither burned nor locked

In this case the verdict is forced to CAUTION minimum, regardless of score.

if (safeBlocked && score >= 800) risk = "CAUTION" // SAFE gate active
