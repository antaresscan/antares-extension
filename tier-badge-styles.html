HOW TO PICK

  1. Want the smallest possible change? Pick 01 — Violet outline pill. Same shape as today, just stops fighting the SAFE colour.
  2. Want zero badge clutter? Pick 06 — Underline or 15 — Brand color shift. The tier signal lives inside the brand, not next to it.
  3. Want premium / luxury feel? Pick 14 — Italic serif or 12 — Brushed metal plate. Pro feels like a different object, not an “activated” one.
  4. Want it to feel like membership / status? Pick 03 — Star count or 16 — Member ID. Communicates progression instead of binary on/off.
  5. Want flashy / collectible? Pick 11 — Holographic gradient. Iridescent rainbow loop — distinctly Pro, distinctly not green.
  6. Want the metaphor of unlock? Pick 10 — Padlock or 19 — Lock + label. Closed → open, no colour-change-on-button.
My pick: 01 — Violet outline pill as the safest swap (one CSS change, ships in minutes), or 03 — Star count if you want a fresh visual that also helps differentiate Free / Pro / Yearly / Lifetime tiers (1 / 3 / 4 / 5 stars). Both avoid the “button turns green” pattern entirely. Tell me which number(s) and I’ll wire it into the production overlay (js/token-app.js) + the design library demo.
